\# Growth Operator — Complete Product Sitemap \& UX Blueprint



This is the detailed sitemap for Growth Operator, from the public website through onboarding, daily operations, content creation, research, engagement, analytics, learning, integrations, and account settings.



The product should feel like an AI Organic Brand Presence Operator: a workspace that helps a business build and maintain its presence across the internet, starting with LinkedIn and expanding to other platforms as authorized capabilities become available.



Important product rule: the sitemap defines the intended experience, not a claim that every feature is currently implemented. Each screen must reflect the actual backend capabilities and data available. Unavailable integrations, metrics, and actions must be clearly marked as unavailable rather than simulated.

\# 18. System-wide UI states and behavior



Every module needs these states; they are part of the sitemap, not optional polish.



\## 18.1 Loading



\* Skeletons that match the final layout



\* No fake numbers



\* No flashing success messages



\* Preserve the current page structure where possible



\## 18.2 Empty state



Every empty state explains:



1\. What this section contains



2\. Why it is currently empty



3\. The next valid action



4\. A direct CTA to that action



Examples:



\* No research sources: “Add a source to begin collecting research.”



\* No opportunities: “Run research or review source documents to identify opportunities.”



\* No drafts: “Choose a content opportunity or start with your own idea.”



\* No published records: “Published records will appear here when actual publication is recorded.”

\* Clear message



\* Affected operation



\* Error category



\* Safe technical details

\# 20. Suggested implementation order



1\. Foundation: app shell, navigation, breadcrumbs, shared page patterns, loading/empty/error states.



2\. Home: clear daily briefing, one primary next action, real activity.



3\. Research: source health, discoveries, source details, topics, gaps, opportunities.



4\. Create: idea-to-draft workflow, complete post editor, evidence, quality checks.



5\. Content: content list, calendar, approvals, publication records.



6\. Approvals: version-bound decisions and approval history.



7\. Connections and sources: transparent permissions and connector status.



8\. Learning and analytics: real recorded outcomes, evidence-backed recommendations.



9\. Engagement and CRM: only to the extent the backend and platform permissions support them.



10\. Settings and production polish: safety policies, audit trail, accessibility, responsive design, security and end-to-end verification.



Final design principle: the user should always know what Growth Operator knows, why it recommends something, what it has actually done, what still needs approval, and what the system cannot currently do.
