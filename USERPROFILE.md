## Writing Samples

### Sample 1: Practical AI and Digital Skills — YFP Founder Voice

**Title:** Practical AI and Digital Skills — YFP Founder Voice

**Content:**

Most beginners don't have an AI tool problem.

They have a direction problem.

Every week, there's a new AI tool promising to change how we work.

One tool writes code. Another builds websites. Another automates tasks.

It's easy to spend hours exploring tools without actually learning how to use them.

Here's a better approach:

Pick one skill you genuinely want to learn.
Choose one tool that helps you practice it.
Build one small, real project.
Understand what works and what doesn't.
Improve your project instead of constantly switching tools.

The real value isn't in knowing the names of 50 AI tools.

It's in knowing how to use a few tools to solve actual problems.

Technology keeps changing. Practical skills help you adapt.

That's what I'm building YourFirstProject around: learning useful digital skills and putting them into action.
