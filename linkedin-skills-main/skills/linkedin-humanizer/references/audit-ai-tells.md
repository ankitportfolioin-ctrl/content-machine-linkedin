# Audit AI Tells: Blacklist & Detection Patterns

## High Priority Blacklist
- Reveal bridges: "The result?", "Here's what happened:", "Plot twist:"
- AI cliches: "delve", "tapestry", "in today's fast-paced world", "testament to"
- Em dash density > 1 per 100 words
- Staccato fragment series > 2 in sequence
