# Worked Audit Examples

### Example 1: B2B Growth Post
**Input:**
"In today's fast-paced world, companies must leverage robust AI frameworks. The result? 300% growth."

**Audit Finding:**
- Fails line 1 cliché: "In today's fast-paced world"
- Fails vocabulary density: "leverage", "robust"
- Fails reveal bridge: "The result?"

**Remediation:**
"68% of enterprise teams missed their pipeline targets in Q1 because their SDR workflows were built on manual research."
