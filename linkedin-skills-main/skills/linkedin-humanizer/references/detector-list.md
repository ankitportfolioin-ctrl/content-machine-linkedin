# Supported AI Detectors & Notes

1. GPTZero - Scans perplexity and burstiness. False positive rate high on non-native English (Stanford 2023).
2. Pangram - High-precision classifier keyed on instruction-tuning signatures.
3. Originality.ai - Commercial content detector.
4. Turnitin - Academic classifier.
5. CopyLeaks - Enterprise classifier.

Note: No detector provides deterministic certification on short LinkedIn posts (100-300 words).
