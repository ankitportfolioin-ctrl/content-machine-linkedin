# Scrub Rules & Regex Patterns

## Density Scoring
Paragraph-level scoring: 3+ markers trigger a rewrite. Single marker in isolation is preserved unless it is forensic leakage.

## Durable 2026 AI Vocabulary Markers
- significant, crucial, notably, particularly, comprehensive, insights, robust, leverage, foster, landscape, nuanced, multifaceted, holistic, streamline, elevate, empower, delve, tapestry, realm, testament.

## Reveal Bridges (Banned)
- "The result?"
- "Here's what changed:"
- "Plot twist:"
- "Here's why:"
- "Let that sink in."
- "It's not X, it's Y."

## Staccato Fragments (Banned Runs)
- "No X. No Y. Just Z."
- "Short. Punchy. Done."
- "Simple. Effective. Easy."
